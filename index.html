22-30 deg C Showers likely

Night tour

Hanging bridges

Canopy views and forest paths around the Arenal area.

Safari boat

Chocolate and coffee

A hands-on food stop between the higher-energy Arenal activities.

Volcano hike

Arenal views, old lava flow terrain, and the walk that anchors this stop.

Rio Fortuna waterfall

The classic waterfall outing near La Fortuna. Expect steps, spray, and a strong finish.
