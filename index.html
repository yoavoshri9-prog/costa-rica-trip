Guide plan

Activities by place

The activity list from Alejandro, arranged by stop with photos that stay close to the Costa Rica landscape and the type of day planned.

1

San Jose

Jul 12 arrival night

The first night is practical: land, clear the airport, and sleep close by before the mountain road begins.

Costa Rica Marriott Hotel Hacienda Belen

700 meters west from Bridgestone/Firestone, La Ribera de Belen, Heredia. Phone: 2298 0000. Marriott page.

2

El Silencio

Jul 13-15, 2 nights at El Silencio Lodge & Spa

Start the Costa Rica leg quietly: lodge trails, water, forest, and the blue-water falls near Bajos del Toro.

Hotel waterfall and hike

Use the lodge setting for a soft landing into the forest before the bigger travel days.

Blue Falls

Blue water, rocks, and a proper walk. Keep this as the main Silencio-area outing.

3

La Fortuna / Arenal

Jul 15-20, 5 nights at Arenal Springs

This is the active middle of the trip: water, canopy, volcano, wildlife, and one slower food/culture day.

Rafting

A river day with enough movement to feel like an adventure, without making every day a rush.

Night tour

Flashlights, frogs, insects, and the forest changing after dark.

Hanging bridges

Canopy views and forest paths around the Arenal area.

Safari boat

A gentler wildlife float for looking, listening, and giving everyone a breather.

Chocolate and coffee

A hands-on food stop between the higher-energy Arenal activities.

Volcano hike

Arenal views, old lava flow terrain, and the walk that anchors this stop.

Rio Fortuna waterfall

The classic waterfall outing near La Fortuna. Expect steps, spray, and a strong finish.

4

Monteverde

Jul 20-22, 2 nights at Koora Monteverde

A short cloud-forest stop, so keep the plan focused: bridges, canopy, and enough time to enjoy the cooler air.

Hanging bridges

Suspension bridges through the cloud forest canopy.

Omega / zip-line

The high-canopy activity for this stop, balanced by a slower evening at Koora.

5

Tamarindo

Jul 22-25, 3 nights at The Coast

The Pacific chapter is surf-first: plan at least two mornings in the water, then keep the afternoons flexible for riding, boat time, and sunset.

Surf lesson day 1

Beginner-friendly water time on the Tamarindo stretch.

Second surf morning

Keep another morning for practice once everyone has the feel of the board.

Horseback riding

A beach ride if the weather, tide, and energy line up.

Catamaran

An easy ocean afternoon: boat, swim stops, and sunset if timing works.

6

Manuel Antonio

Jul 25-28, 3 nights at Parador Resort & Spa

The finale stays close to the water and park: one wildlife morning, one boat option, one faster water option, and real beach time.

National park

The classic Manuel Antonio morning: trails, beach, and wildlife watching.

Catamaran

A coast-from-the-water day if the sea and schedule cooperate.

Jetski

The faster water activity for anyone who wants a burst of speed.

Beach time

No overplanning here. Leave room to swim, sit, read, and do nothing.
